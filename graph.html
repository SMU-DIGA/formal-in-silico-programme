Proof dependencies

Dependency graph

Which ledger claims and which Lean theorems are built on which, read from the Lean proof terms themselves rather than from the ledger’s hand-written dependency column. 387 claims and 8975 theorems; 1632 uses between claims, 17494 between theorems.

    How to read it

    • a claim or theorem, coloured by domain
    • uses — points at what the proof relies on
    • bigger — used by more results
    • what the selection uses
    • what uses the selection

    Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, drag a node to move it; click a node to see what it uses and what uses it, and “Only its chain” to keep just those, directly or not. An arrow from claim A to claim B means that a proof behind A uses a theorem behind B (through lemmas that belong to no claim); two claims can use each other. Claims without a Lean theorem (tests, T0–T2) are not drawn. Generated by scripts/depgraph.py; a reading aid, not part of the trust chain.
