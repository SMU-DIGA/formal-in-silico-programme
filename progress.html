Progress

What the programme has achieved and what it plans. Stages I–III belong to Phase I, certified correctness; Phase II, AI-powered unification, comes last and has not started. Stage by stage: Stage I (reached, frozen on 30 October 2026), Stage II (under way until 29 January 2027: making DFT and CALPHAD usable for research) and Stage III (candidates). Each domain has its own page with the item-by-item status. A feature counts as done only when it is implemented, agrees with the reference code, and every number it outputs is covered by a Lean theorem.

Stage I (Sept – Oct 2026): reached, frozen at tag stage-1 on 30 Oct 2026

Stage I established machine-checked triple correctness on certified discrete problems and used it to audit widely used DFT codes and CALPHAD databases. Its claims keep their statements, tiers and pins; later work extends them only through new claims. The three goals and the charter’s five phases come first, then the headline results; the proof of several exact Kohn–Sham solutions of one discrete problem is described on the DFT page.

G1 — mini-DFT reaches DFTK parity set 29 Sep 2026 Partial

10 of 46 items done and 2 more agree with DFTK. In one dimension the smearings (Fermi–Dirac, Gaussian, Marzari–Vanderbilt and Methfessel–Paxton of any order), LDA and collinear spin (LSDA, with PW92 correlation) are complete: SCF-solution certificates at finite and zero temperature, at the Γ point and with Monkhorst–Pack k-points, with bands and DOS, all bound to the named external potential (to 8 Oct). Forces on Gaussian sites and closed-shell Hartree–Fock are partly done (8 Oct; the Hartree–Fock energy interval is not yet certified). Since 16 October the tracker also covers the 3D work: real silicon, AlP and GaP with GTH pseudopotentials and LDA (headline results below). Item-by-item status →

G2 — certified finite elements against scikit-fem set 30 Sep 2026 Partial

17 of 27 items done and 1 more agrees with scikit-fem. One dimension is complete in F0: Poisson with P1 elements and beams with cubic Hermite elements, certified against the true solution, Neumann at both ends (5 Oct) and certified discrete eigenvalues (6 Oct). Since 14 October there are guaranteed error bounds in two dimensions (below). Item-by-item status →

G3 — certified CALPHAD against pycalphad set 1 Oct 2026 Partial

21 of 28 items done and 1 more agrees with pycalphad. C0 (binary point equilibrium) is complete. By 6 October: certified phase diagrams with a partition certificate per temperature, the Inden–Hillert–Jarl magnetic model, driving forces, the checker reading the TDB file itself, convexity certificates, sublattice phases with one or two mixing sublattices (Laves phases among them), equilibria at a given chemical potential or activity, and G, S, H, Cp of a phase. Since 14 October also eutectic and eutectoid temperatures (CAL-B15, B24), phase diagrams over temperature intervals (CAL-C12) and interstitial phases and magnetic compounds (CAL-C22–C24). Multicomponent systems, further invariant types and order–disorder continue in Stage II. Item-by-item status →

  1. PHASE 0Minimal DFTDone · 6 Oct

    Trust ledger, Lean certificate checkers, trust reports and trace validation are in place; all four benchmark groups pass, including the DFTK cross-check.

    Done 5 Octcontinuum well-posedness, the a priori plane-wave estimate and linear-mixing convergence reached their target T1 as paper proofs; no published result covers the 1D periodic Hartree kernel directly, and the mixing condition was corrected to α < 2/λmax of the dielectric operator.

    Done 6 Octdecision D1 chose Frama-C’s floating-point model: the interval arithmetic is proved correct on the C code (DFT-F01, F02 at T4) and the Lean–Rocq interface claim is withdrawn, so every obligation is at its target. The cutoff-limit claim DFT-C02 was carried into later phases and reached T3 on 8 October: the truncation limit E∞ equals the ground energy of the continuum model, proved in Lean with the continuum model written in Fourier space (its equivalence with the operator model on H¹ is assumption A-DFT-08).

  2. PHASE 1DFT extensionsPartial

    k-point sampling is implemented and certified; the thread-parallel k-point protocol is model-checked and runs are bitwise identical across thread counts. Beyond the original plan: finite-temperature occupations with free-energy, entropy and energy certificates, Anderson mixing, non-self-consistent bands and DOS, and the truncation-error certificate.

    LDASlater exchange and PW92 correlation certified at the Γ point and with k-points (equivalent supercell) with Fermi–Dirac, Gaussian, MV or MP smearing, and since 6 October at zero temperature under a spectral gap.

    Since done3D plane waves with GTH pseudopotentials are certified for Si (from 10 Oct), AlP and GaP; since 28 October an MPI driver of the 3D point-group LDA problem (showcase S3) is bitwise identical in 25 tested layouts with between 1 and 32 processes and its logs pass trace validation (DFT-R13–R18).

    Not yetthe exit criterion asks for bitwise-identical runs on 1 to 64 processes.

  3. PHASE 2MD and FEMDone · 1 Oct

    Both minimal versions print trust reports. mini-FEM: 1D Poisson (P1) and beams (cubic Hermite), certified against the true solution. mini-MD v0 (1 Oct 2026): a 1D Lennard-Jones chain with velocity Verlet, certified against the true trajectory. Céa’s lemma (FEM-D03) and Verlet symplecticity (MD-D03) are both at T3.

    Since donea 3D periodic box for MD (14 Oct, MD-C09–C13) and 2D triangle meshes for FEM (14 Oct, FEM-D10–D18).

    Not startedthe TLA+ modules for halo exchange, atom migration, parallel assembly and checkpointing; thermostats for MD.

  4. PHASE 3Trusted kernelsPartial

    Done 6 Octthe interval-arithmetic kernel of the DFT driver — charge interval, residual bound and inertia count — is proved on the C code with Frama-C/WP: no run-time errors (DFT-F03) and, since 6 October, floating-point correctness under an IEEE 754 model (DFT-F01, F02), all at T4.

    Not yetthe three kernels the charter names for this phase: the plane-wave kinetic operator, FEM element-stiffness assembly and one Verlet step.

  5. PHASE 4BenchmarkingPartial

    Donemanufactured-solution studies for FEM with certified error bounds (showcase and tutorial); certified NVE energy drift in MD (ENERGY_DRIFT, MD-B03); and established codes run against certified answers under protocols frozen in advance — ABINIT, Quantum ESPRESSO and DFTK (22 Oct, DFT-O25–O27) and pycalphad on published databases (from 4 Oct; headline results below).

    Not yetthe Δ-gauge for DFT and radial distribution functions for MD.

CALPHAD is not one of the charter’s original phases; it was added as goal G3 on 1 October 2026 and follows the same completion standard as G1 and G2.

Stage II (30 Oct 2026 – 29 Jan 2027): make DFT and CALPHAD usable for research

A researcher brings their own problem — their crystal structure, pseudopotential and settings in DFT, their own database and conditions in CALPHAD — and gets, on one workstation and within a fixed time, a Lean-checked certificate for the stated discrete problem or a refusal that says exactly why not. FEM, MD and CSP stay as they are. Progress is measured against 24 acceptance tasks that were fixed, with their input files and hashes, on 30 October 2026, before any Stage II development.

The acceptance tasks are judged formally at the end of the stage by a fresh agent who reads only the public documentation. “Done” here means that the capability exists and has been checked, not that the acceptance run has passed.
